Token Sharing Phase 1

Share Verified Case Data With Your AiPrise Partners

Token Sharing lets a Donor company share the end merchant input data behind a verified business or user with a Recipient company. The Recipient imports the token and reruns those data on its own templates, so your customer is not asked for the same data twice.

01 / Connect
Form a contract

The Recipient invites the Donor. Once the Donor accepts, the contract shows as Active on both sides. An active contract shares no data. It only allows agreements to be proposed.

Recipient and DonorInvite a partner and accept the contract
02 / Agree
Set the terms

The Recipient drafts an agreement. The Donor reviews each section and accepts or declines it with a reason.

RecipientBuild an agreement in five steps
DonorReview, decline or approve an agreement
03 / Share
Export and import

Once live, eligible cases are exported as input tokens. The Recipient imports them and runs its own templates.

Donor and RecipientExport a token and import it
04 / Request
Ask for more

The Recipient can request extra questions or documents, limited to the RFI catalog both sides agreed on.

Recipient and DonorRequest and answer additional information
05 / Control
Pause, resume or end sharing

Either side can pause or terminate a live agreement. Resuming needs both sides to agree.

Donor and RecipientPause, resume or terminate an agreement
