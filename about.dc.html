Token Sharing

Verify a Customer Once. Share the Data With Your Partners.

Token Sharing enables two AiPrise customers who serve the same business or user to securely share verified data from an existing case. The receiving partner can choose to accept the shared verification as-is or re-run specific checks based on their own risk appetite and compliance requirements.

The use case

One Customer, Many Regulated Partners

A merchant that signs up with a payments platform often also needs an account with that platform's banking, settlement or lending partner. Each partner has its own compliance obligations, so the merchant is asked for the same registration documents, ownership details and addresses several times.

With Token Sharing, the company that already verified the merchant shares those inputs under an agreed set of rules. The partner reruns them through its own AiPrise templates. Each company keeps its own policies, results and decisions.

Without Token Sharing
The merchant uploads the same documents to every partner.
Each partner collects and checks the data from scratch.
Onboarding with the second partner starts only after the first is done.
With Token Sharing
The merchant is verified once by the first partner.
Approved cases that match the agreement are shared as tokens.
The partner runs its own checks and asks only for what is missing.

Key terms

The Words You Will See

Contract

A connection between a Donor and a Recipient. It allows agreements to be proposed. It does not share any data.

Agreement

The rules for sharing under a contract: which cases qualify, from when to when, and what may be requested later. The Donor approves it before anything is shared.

Token

A package of one approved case's input data, sent from Donor to Recipient under a live agreement.

Export

The Donor side of a token. Approved cases that match the agreement are queued and sent automatically.

Import

The Recipient side of a token. It is pulled in automatically or by hand, creating a new profile.

RFI

A request for information. The Recipient asks for a question or document that is in the agreement's catalog.

Example scenarios

Where Token Sharing Fits

Illustrative setups based on common partner models. Company types are examples.

01 / Payments and settlement
A payments platform shares merchant KYB with its settlement partner
DonorCross-border payments platform
RecipientSettlement partner
AgreementBusiness Profiles · Indonesia · not crypto · max 6 months old · automatic import

Approved merchants arrive at the partner as tokens and run through its own KYB template. A bank statement request is answered from the platform's data on file.

02 / Marketplace lending
A marketplace shares seller KYB with a working-capital lender
DonorB2B marketplace
RecipientLending partner
AgreementBusiness Profiles · sellers who opt in · manual import

The lender reviews each token before importing. Its source-of-funds question goes to the seller under the marketplace's name.

03 / Wallet and card issuing
A wallet app shares user KYC with its card-issuing partner
DonorConsumer wallet
RecipientCard issuer
AgreementUser Profiles · approved users · automatic import

Users who request a card do not repeat identity steps. The issuer runs its own KYC template on the shared inputs.
